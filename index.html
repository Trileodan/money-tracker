Build 102 · cash flow by day

Your monthly income

Set your take-home pay, how often you're paid, and when. Add a new dated figure whenever your pay changes — earlier months keep the figure that applied then.

Credit card

Mark a payment "Paid on credit card" when adding it. It counts toward the category straight away, but only affects your left-to-spend when the card payment leaves — a month in arrears (all of a month's card spend is paid on your card day the following month).

Your card's billing cycle — used by the Cash Flow tab to know when your card payment leaves.

Your data

Stored privately on this device only — nothing is uploaded. A second protected copy is kept automatically and restored if your browser ever clears its storage.

Install on your phone

iPhone: open this file in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen".

Android: open in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then "Add to Home screen".

It then opens full-screen like a normal app and works offline.

Balance, target & transfers

Current balance

Enter your savings balance every so often, dated. Anything you log under the Savings category after that date is added on automatically.

Savings target

The amount you want your savings to reach. We use your monthly Savings category target to estimate how long it'll take.

Move savings to spending

Move money out of savings into this month's spendable income. It lowers your savings, adds it to your income, and lifts your flexible spending budget for the month.

Your details & inputs

Your details

Current portfolio total

Submit your actual portfolio value now and then — projections start from your most recent figure. Add a new one whenever the estimate drifts from reality.

Rate of growth

Enter how much your investment grew (or fell) over the previous 12 months — e.g. 7 for +7%, or -5 for a 5% fall. This is used as the annual rate of growth in the projection.

Last 12 months

Your monthly grade for each of the past 12 months. Tap a month to see what made up the score.

Streaks

Track habits like "no takeaways" or "add to savings weekly". Each box shows your current run — tap it for best-ever, last broken, edit or delete.
